Data capture

Skilled workers wear a light head camera and do their normal job, in their own workplace or at our recording sites, following the capture plan we agree with you.

Data processing

Our review team in Manila checks every recorded hour, blurs faces and voices, and labels and prepares the footage for learning, so it arrives in the structure your capture plan specifies.

Release testing

Before a release reaches your fleet, we run it on physical robots in hotels, offices, kitchens and plants, against the tasks and evidence you agree with us, and report exactly how it performs.

Our company

Anyô Robotics is a robotics deployment company, headquartered in Singapore and operating from the Philippines. We put robots to work in real workplaces and give them what labs cannot: skilled people, verified real-world data and a human on hand for every shift.

Through a network of working sites, from hotels, offices and malls to warehouses, factories and plants, we record how skilled people do their jobs, test robot software on physical robots, supervise fleets from our operations centre in Manila and deploy robots for businesses. One operation connects it all, so every shift in the field makes the next robot better.

Our partners

Partner groups across co-living, hotels, offices, food and beverage, malls and gyms open their people and sites to Anyô, invest in the company and are first in line for our robots.

Our team

Anyô was founded by Gabby Dizon, Mark Kooijman and Janna de Guzman. Between them they have built, scaled and exited businesses across Asia, and now bring that operating discipline to robotics.

Our vision

We want the Philippines to be where the world's robots learn to work, and Filipinos to be the people who teach, run and maintain them, earning more and building new careers along the way.
